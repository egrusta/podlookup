## Important status
PodLookup is currently a demonstration storefront. No supplier, merchant account, product stock, age-verification provider, recycling contract or commercial fulfilment arrangement is approved or connected. Checkout must remain disabled until all applicable gates are evidenced and signed off.

Review official sources and provider terms again immediately before any commercial launch.
