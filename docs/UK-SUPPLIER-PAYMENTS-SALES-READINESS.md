# PodLookup — supplier, payments and sales readiness pack

**Research date:** 4 November 2026  
**Current operating status:** DEMONSTRATION ONLY — no real orders, payments, stock or supplier relationship is currently confirmed.  
**Decision:** HOLD all checkout and commercial claims until the gates in this document are evidenced and signed off.

This is a working business-readiness document, not legal, tax, financial or regulatory advice. Supplier and payment-provider statements below are public claims, not an audit or endorsement. Verify current terms directly and obtain UK specialist advice before trading.

## 1. Recommended launch model

### Phase 0 — trusted compatibility information centre (now)
- Keep PodLookup as a factual, evidence-led compatibility reference.
- Keep all basket/checkout controls in demo mode. No payment collection, order acceptance, fake stock, fake ratings or delivery promises.
- Keep the product catalogue clearly labelled as illustrative until a supplier, genuine stock feed, prices, fulfilment and returns process are contracted and tested.
- Build useful device/platform pages from manufacturer evidence. Preserve UNKNOWN where compatibility is not verified.
- Do not use paid product ads, promotional email/SMS, influencer or affiliate campaigns for nicotine-containing vape products without specialist legal review. Search discovery should be driven by factual, neutral compatibility information rather than promotional claims.

### Phase 1 — small, vetted UK catalogue
Start with a narrow list of refillable devices and replacement pods that PodLookup can evidence. Prefer UK-based, duty-paid stock and a supplier that can provide traceable invoices, authenticity/provenance evidence and reliable fulfilment. Do not import, manufacture, rebrand, hold duty-suspended stock or act as an overseas manufacturer's representative in the first phase.

A dropship arrangement is not a transfer of the retailer's legal responsibility. The customer-facing seller must still ensure the products it offers are lawful, age checks work, customer terms are accurate, and records/returns/recalls are handled.

### Phase 2 — controlled sales
Only after written supplier approval, payment underwriting, age verification, product checks, policies and end-to-end tests:
1. Supplier stock and cost are confirmed.
2. Product SKU and compatibility evidence are approved.
3. Checkout performs age/identity checks before accepting an order.
4. Payment provider has explicitly approved the vape product category and the exact business model in writing.
5. Supplier confirms fulfilment, tracking, neutral packaging, failed-age delivery handling and returns/recalls.
6. A test order, refund, failed age check and cancellation all pass before launch.

### Supplier approval evidence — mandatory before first SKU
Request and file all of the following:
- Legal business name, registered/trading address, company number (if incorporated), VAT number (if applicable), named account contact and bank-account payee name.
- Trade terms, dropship agreement, full price list, all fees, minimums, payment terms, dispatch cut-off, service levels and delivery zones.
- Written confirmation of the exact fulfilment model: who is seller of record, who takes payment, who owns stock, who dispatches, who handles lost parcels, failed age checks, returns, refunds, complaints and recalls.
- Proof of brand/manufacturer or authorised-distributor supply chain for each brand and SKU; invoices/provenance where commercially appropriate.
- Product/SKU identifiers, manufacturer/importer details, UK market status, packaging/leaflet photographs, batch/lot traceability, recall contact and product-safety/compliance declarations.
- For products requiring MHRA notification, exact notification/listing references and confirmation that the product is published on the relevant MHRA list. Separately inspect the real packaging: notification alone does not guarantee compliant labelling.
- Written confirmation that stock is reusable and not a banned single-use vape. No disposable or single-use products may enter the catalogue.
- Vaping Products Duty status for liable e-liquid products: evidence the duty has been accounted for and the correct duty stamp is on the outermost retail packaging where required. If unstamped transitional stock is offered, obtain evidence it was produced/imported before 1 October 2026 and is within the statutory transition ending 31 March 2027. Do not accept unexplained unstamped stock.
- Product liability insurance details and claims/recall process; confirm whether PodLookup is covered or needs its own policy.
- Stock availability feed/API/CSV, product images and permission to use them, data update frequency and an out-of-stock procedure.
- Neutral packaging, no supplier marketing inserts, tracked delivery, age-at-delivery procedure, delivery failure and return-to-sender handling.
- Returns/warranty process for faulty electrical goods, unopened/used pods and hygiene-related exceptions; clarify who pays each return cost.
- Data-processing terms if the supplier receives customer names, addresses, emails or phone numbers; secure transfer and deletion/retention arrangements.
- Two sample orders (before public launch): one normal test delivery and one controlled failed/undeliverable age-check test, plus a refund/return test.

**Reject immediately** if the supplier cannot explain provenance, refuses invoices, cannot identify the manufacturer/importer, offers single-use vapes, asks for payment to an unrelated personal account, cannot evidence duty status, or pressures PodLookup to publish claims before documents are supplied.

## 3. Payment provider shortlist — written pre-approval required

**Do not assume Stripe or Shopify Payments will accept vape sales.** Stripe's current UK restricted-business list explicitly includes tobacco products including e-cigarettes and e-liquid. Do not open a normal Stripe account and attempt to process vape transactions under a different category.

The Independent British Vape Trade Association (IBVTA) currently lists the following providers for vape businesses. IBVTA expressly says it does not endorse, approve or control them, so this is a lead list, not a guarantee of approval.

| [WorldNet via IBVTA](https://www.ibvta.org.uk/online-payment-gateways/) | IBVTA page describes UK/Ireland vape-business payment services, 3-D Secure, fraud screening, virtual terminal and ecommerce integrations. | Written UK vape-category approval and confirmation of support for a custom static/JavaScript storefront; gateway, acquiring bank, settlement, reserves and full fee schedule. |

### Questions every payment provider must answer in writing
1. Do you accept a UK online retailer selling reusable vape devices and replacement pods/coils? Do you accept nicotine-free and nicotine-containing product categories? What about e-liquid?
2. Are any product types or sales channels excluded? Are dropshipped products accepted, and does the supplier need to be disclosed/underwritten?
3. What business documents, supplier invoices, MHRA evidence, duty-stamp evidence, age-verification evidence and website policies are required?
4. What is the full cost: setup, monthly minimum, gateway, transaction percentage, fixed fee, authorisation, refund, chargeback, PCI, 3-D Secure, cross-border and termination fees?
5. Is there a rolling reserve, delayed settlement, deposit or minimum processing history? How long are funds held?
6. Which acquiring bank/card schemes actually underwrite the account? Who is the contractual provider?
7. Can it integrate with PodLookup's current custom website without migrating to Shopify/WooCommerce? Provide API/hosted checkout documentation and test environment.
8. Can refunds, partial refunds, cancellations, disputes, Apple Pay/Google Pay and 3-D Secure be supported?
9. What triggers account review, suspension or reserve changes? How are complaints and urgent payment incidents handled?

**Payment decision:** no provider selected or connected. Compare written quotes only after supplier/product scope and the legal seller entity are known. Never misdescribe the business category or hide vape products from underwriting.

## 4. Sales and customer acquisition — compliant route

### What PodLookup can build now
- Factual compatibility pages with model names, platform distinctions, supported resistance options and manufacturer source links.
- A searchable finder that helps an adult visitor identify the correct pod platform without implying a product is in stock or for sale.
- Neutral comparison tables limited to objective specifications and verified fit.
- Clear “last checked” dates, correction/contact route and explicit UNKNOWN labels.
- Search-engine technical hygiene: accurate sitemap/robots/metadata, useful non-promotional help pages and structured information that does not invent ratings or availability.

### What must not be launched casually
- Paid search/display/social ads or promotional video ads for nicotine-containing vape products.
- Influencer, affiliate, referral, coupon, loyalty, giveaway or promotional email/SMS campaigns without specialist review.
- “Best”, “top”, “market-leading”, health/safety/cessation claims, unsupported comparisons, fake testimonials or invented review scores.
- Free product giveaways or substantial promotional discounts/coupons intended to promote vaping/nicotine products.
- Any youth-appealing creative, imagery, language, game-like promotion or audience targeting.

ASA/CAP guidance says nicotine-containing e-cigarettes and components are prohibited from promotion in most online/electronic media, with limited scope for factual, non-promotional content on a marketer's own website. Section 22 rules also apply to vape products/components regardless of nicotine content. Have every proposed sales or marketing campaign reviewed before release.

### Practical sales model to evaluate
- **First:** operate as a factual compatibility resource while we validate suppliers and legal requirements.
- **Then:** if cleared, sell a small UK-only range with supplier-fulfilled orders, genuine traceable stock, real prices and tested age verification.
- Do not rely on affiliate links as a workaround for advertising restrictions; the content and commercial relationship still need review.
- Do not expand into imports, own-brand products, liquids or cross-border sales in the initial launch.

### HMRC records and duty — important 2026 change
HMRC guidance says Vaping Products Duty applies to vaping liquid, including liquid in bottles, cartridges and pods, whether or not it contains nicotine. The published rate is £2.20 per 10 ml. Duty stamps must be affixed to the outermost retail packaging where required. Retailers selling/distributing only duty-paid products do not need to apply for Vaping Products Duty or the stamp scheme approval themselves, but must retain evidence that products are legitimate.

Keep for at least six years: supplier name/address/contact details, invoices, delivery notes and dates received, product quantities bought/held/supplied/sold, production/import dates, and checks made to establish legitimacy. Unstamped pre-1 October 2026 stock has a transition only through 31 March 2027 if the conditions are evidenced. Do not rely on verbal assurances.
