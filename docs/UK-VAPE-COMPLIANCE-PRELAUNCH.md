# PodLookup — UK vape retail pre-launch compliance checklist

**Reviewed:** 4 November 2026  
**Status:** Planning checklist only. PodLookup is currently a demonstration storefront: it does not sell products, take payments, or place orders.

This document is an operational checklist, not legal advice. The rules below are time-sensitive. Before enabling checkout, confirm the current requirements with the relevant Trading Standards service and a UK solicitor or specialist compliance adviser.

## 1. Age of sale and customer checks

- [ ] Do not enable sales until an age-verification process is in place and tested.
- [ ] From **29 October 2026**, the age-of-sale restriction applies to **all vaping products**, including nicotine-free products and related components such as pods and coils. Retailers, including online retailers, must not sell to anyone under 18.
- [ ] Prevent proxy purchasing: an adult must not buy, or attempt to buy, a vaping or nicotine product on behalf of someone under 18.
- [ ] Define a documented process for checking age when there is doubt. Confirm the current detailed verification regulations for the relevant UK nation before launch; Scotland has specific age-verification policy requirements.
- [ ] Ensure customer support and fulfilment staff know when to pause or refuse an order.

Official source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-vaping-and-nicotine-products

## 2. Product legality and supplier evidence

- [ ] Sell only reusable products that meet the current UK definition. The single-use vape ban has applied since **1 June 2025** to all vapes, with or without nicotine.
- [ ] For each device, confirm the rechargeable battery, refillable container and (where fitted) removable/replacement coil requirements.
- [ ] Keep supplier invoices, manufacturer identity, product identifiers, batch/lot details, authenticity evidence and written product-compliance documentation.
- [ ] For e-cigarettes and e-liquids, verify the relevant MHRA notification is published before supply. A listing on a notified-products list does not, by itself, prove all labelling and other requirements are met.
- [ ] Check applicable TRPR limits and packaging/labelling requirements for each product category, including nicotine concentration and tank/refill sizes.
- [ ] Maintain a product-level evidence record. If authenticity, legality or compatibility cannot be established, do not list the item as verified.

Official sources:
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/single-use-vapes-ban
- https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/advice-for-retailers-and-producers

## 3. Promotions and discounts

- [ ] Do not run free giveaways or promotional free distribution of vaping/nicotine products or coupons.
- [ ] Do not offer substantial promotional discounts on these products or coupons.
- [ ] Check branded accessories, gifts and bundles too: the restrictions can also cover other items whose purpose or effect is to promote vaping/nicotine products.
- [ ] Have any proposed promotion reviewed before publication. Normal pricing and discounting are fact-specific; do not assume every price reduction is permitted.

The relevant restrictions came into force on **29 October 2026**. The official guidance describes limited exceptions, including arrangements with a public authority.

Official source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/selling-vaping-and-nicotine-products

## 4. Website content and advertising

- [ ] Keep compatibility pages factual, neutral and evidence-led. State the device, pod platform, fit evidence and uncertainty without promotional claims.
- [ ] For unlicensed nicotine-containing e-cigarettes and their components, do not assume that a product page on the retailer's own site can be promotional advertising. The CAP guidance describes a limited scope for factual, non-promotional information on the marketer's own website.
- [ ] Do not launch paid online display ads, paid search listings or algorithmically distributed promotional social posts for nicotine-containing vape products without specialist review; these are among the media restrictions described by ASA/CAP.
- [ ] Review email, SMS, affiliate, influencer and third-party promotion plans separately before use.
- [ ] Avoid health, safety, cessation or medicinal claims unless specifically permitted and substantiated under the applicable rules.
- [ ] Do not target under-18s; review imagery, wording, placement and audience composition.

Official ASA/CAP sources:
- https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/electronic-cigarettes-factual-vs-promotional-claims.html
- https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/electronic-cigarettes-media-prohibitions.html
- https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/22.html

## 5. PodLookup trust controls

- [ ] Keep the demo notice visible until real checkout, stock, delivery, returns and support processes are operational.
- [ ] Do not display illustrative prices, ratings, delivery promises or stock status as if they were live commercial facts.
- [ ] Require supplier provenance and authenticity evidence before marking any product “genuine” or “verified”.
- [ ] Keep compatibility evidence separate from supplier/product authenticity evidence: a pod can fit a device without its seller or supply chain being trustworthy.
- [ ] Preserve the **UNKNOWN** state whenever fit or authenticity is not established. Never infer compatibility from appearance or brand alone.
- [ ] Publish clear business identity, contact, delivery, returns, privacy and complaints information before taking orders.

## Launch gate

**Do not switch on payments or accept orders** until the age-check flow, supplier verification, product legality records, prohibited-product screening, promotional controls, customer terms and fulfilment process have all been reviewed and tested.

Last reviewed against the official sources linked above on 4 November 2026. Re-check those sources immediately before launch and whenever the law or guidance changes.
