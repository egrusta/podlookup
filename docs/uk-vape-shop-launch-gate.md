# PodLookup — UK vape shop launch compliance gate

**Review date:** 4 November 2026  
**Status:** **HOLD — do not enable checkout or accept orders until every launch gate below is signed off.**  
**Scope:** UK online retail of reusable vape devices, refillable pods/cartridges, coils and e-liquids.

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Before selling, have the final product range, checkout, marketing and fulfilment process reviewed against the current rules by a suitably qualified UK regulatory adviser and, where appropriate, the relevant Trading Standards authority. Re-check official guidance immediately before launch.

## 1. Product and supplier approval

- [ ] Use identifiable UK-compliant suppliers with invoices and a traceable business address.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation that every device, pod, cartridge, coil and e-liquid is genuine and intended for the UK market.
- [ ] For every nicotine-containing e-cigarette or refill container, verify that the exact brand and product notification is published on the MHRA notified-product list before accepting supply.
- [ ] Keep the MHRA notification reference, manufacturer/importer details, product specification, packaging photographs and supplier invoice against each SKU.
- [ ] Check that the actual packaging and leaflet comply with UK requirements. MHRA notification alone does not guarantee compliant packaging.
- [ ] Keep batch/lot identifiers, supplier recall contact details and a documented process to suspend a listing, contact affected customers and process a recall.
- [ ] Do not list a product if its identity, UK compliance status, authenticity or supplier provenance is unclear.

**Product limits to verify for each relevant nicotine product:**

- [ ] Maximum nicotine concentration: **20 mg/ml**.
- [ ] Maximum tank/cartridge capacity: **2 ml**.
- [ ] Maximum refill-container volume: **10 ml**.
- [ ] Required child-resistant/tamper-evident features, ingredient and nicotine information, health warnings, instructions/leaflet and producer contact details are present where required.
- [ ] Do not stock or sell single-use vapes. The UK single-use vape ban has applied since 1 June 2025.
- [ ] Treat nicotine-free vapes as age-restricted products too. Do not assume that “0 mg” removes the age-of-sale or general product-safety obligations.

## 2. Age of sale and age verification

Since **29 October 2026**, the age-of-sale restriction covers **all vaping products and nicotine products**, including nicotine-free vapes and related components such as pods and coils. Online retailers must not sell to anyone under 18. It is also an offence for an adult to buy, or attempt to buy, these products on behalf of an under-18.

- [ ] Apply an 18+ gate to every in-scope product, not only nicotine-containing e-liquids.
- [ ] Use a robust age/identity verification process before the sale is accepted. A date-of-birth field, tick-box or customer declaration alone is not a suitable control.
- [ ] Refuse the sale if age cannot be established or there is reason to suspect proxy purchasing.
- [ ] Train anyone handling orders or customer support to recognise and refuse suspected proxy purchases.
- [ ] Decide how age is checked at delivery and what happens when the recipient cannot prove they are 18+.
- [ ] Keep a written age-verification policy, staff training record, refusal process and proportionate audit trail.
- [ ] Minimise personal data: prefer an age-verification provider that returns a pass/fail or age token; do not retain full identity-document images unless there is a clear lawful need and retention policy.
- [ ] For Scotland, operate a formal age-verification policy and have regard to the Scottish Government retailer guidance.
- [ ] Before 1 January 2027, review the England and Wales age-verification regulations and any updated GOV.UK guidance. The draft 2026 regulations describe physical photo ID with date of birth and a holographic/UV feature, or qualifying digital identification through a Digital Verification Service. Confirm the final in-force requirements before relying on a statutory defence.

## 3. Promotions, advertising and product claims

From **29 October 2026**, promotional free distribution and substantial promotional discounts/coupons for vaping or nicotine products are restricted where the purpose or effect is to promote the products. This is not a blanket ban on every ordinary price reduction; assess each offer against the legislation and current guidance.

- [ ] Do not offer free vape devices, pods, coils, e-liquids or samples as promotional gifts.
- [ ] Do not use promotional coupons or substantial promotional discounts without a documented legal review.
- [ ] Review bundles, loyalty rewards, affiliate codes, referral rewards, giveaways and clearance campaigns before publishing.
- [ ] Obtain specialist review before running paid online advertising, influencer/affiliate promotion or product-led social campaigns. Existing restrictions on advertising nicotine-containing e-cigarettes remain relevant.
- [ ] Do not make health, safety, cessation, “risk-free”, “safe”, comparative-harm or unsupported environmental claims.
- [ ] Keep compatibility statements factual and traceable to manufacturer evidence. Never describe an UNKNOWN fit as compatible.
- [ ] Check product names, images and packaging presentation against the rules on misleading or promotional presentation.

## 4. Website and checkout controls

- [ ] Keep the current demo storefront clearly labelled as a demonstration until the shop is legally and operationally ready.
- [ ] Do not activate payments, order submission or stock claims before supplier and compliance approval.
- [ ] Integrate age verification before the order can be accepted, and define the delivery-age-check process.
- [ ] Prevent under-18 accounts/orders and provide a clear refusal path for failed or inconclusive checks.
- [ ] Publish accurate Terms and Conditions, privacy notice, cookie information, delivery policy, returns/refunds policy, complaints process and recall contact details.
- [ ] Make clear which listings are compatibility information and which are actually available for purchase.
- [ ] Do not display prices, delivery promises, stock levels or “in stock” statements unless they are backed by a real supplier/fulfilment process.
- [ ] Ensure payment, order, customer-service and age-verification providers have been assessed for data protection, security and contractual suitability.
- [ ] Test failed age checks, abandoned orders, chargebacks, refunds, returns, recall notices and customer-data deletion before launch.

## 5. PodLookup-specific trust controls

- [ ] Preserve the verified compatibility dataset and source links for every device/pod relationship.
- [ ] Keep device-supported resistance options separate from pod/cartridge resistance options and replaceable-coil resistance options.
- [ ] Keep model-specific restrictions visible (for example, XLIM 0.4Ω Top Fill and Caliburn A2S 1.2Ω side-refill limitations).
- [ ] Keep UNKNOWN values as UNKNOWN until an official manufacturer source resolves them.
- [ ] Do not imply that a compatibility record means a product is stocked, genuine, MHRA-notified or available to buy. Those are separate checks.
- [ ] Keep a dated source-review record and re-check manufacturer compatibility information when a model or cartridge is revised.

## 6. Launch sign-off

The shop stays in demo mode until each owner has supplied evidence and signed off:

| Gate | Required evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier and authenticity | Supplier due-diligence pack, invoices, provenance | NOT SIGNED OFF |
| Product compliance | SKU-by-SKU MHRA/product-safety and packaging review | NOT SIGNED OFF |
| Age verification | Tested 18+ flow, refusal handling, policy and records | NOT SIGNED OFF |
| Promotions and advertising | Written review of offers, ads, affiliate and social content | NOT SIGNED OFF |
| Checkout and fulfilment | Payments, delivery, age-at-delivery, returns and recalls tested | NOT SIGNED OFF |
| Privacy and security | Privacy/cookie notices, data retention and provider review | NOT SIGNED OFF |
| Final legal review | Qualified UK regulatory review / Trading Standards queries resolved | NOT SIGNED OFF |

**Release rule:** no real orders, payment capture or “available to buy” claims until every applicable gate is signed off and the final review date is recorded.
