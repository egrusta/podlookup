Pod compatibility, explained

Which pods fit which device — and why the answer depends on the pod platform, not the brand.

PodLookup is the compatibility reference: a plain-English answer to the question of which pods fit your device. In a refillable pod system the pod holds the coil and the e-liquid, and every device runs one pod platform, so what matters is not the brand on the battery but the platform your device is recorded on. The UK reference covers three of the most common refillable families. The Vaporesso XROS range — XROS 3, XROS 4, XROS 4 Mini, XROS 5 and XROS 5 Mini — all share the XROS Pods platform, so the same replacement pods fit each one. OXVA XLIM devices, the Xlim SE, Xlim Pro, Xlim Pro 2 and Xlim SQ Pro, share the Xlim Pods platform. Uwell Caliburn is the one to check carefully: the Caliburn G and G2 share one platform, the Caliburn G3 has its own, and the Caliburn A3 takes A3 pods. Every statement here comes from our recorded compatibility data; where a device or pod is not recorded, we say so rather than guess.
